# Frontend delivery phases

## Phase 1 — discovery and public buying journey

- React/Vite structure, shared design system, responsive header and mobile navigation
- English/Urdu switch, RTL direction, translated interface and local persistence
- Onboarding through `POST /users`, saved user and role
- Market selection, crop search/category filtering and latest prices
- Refresh behavior, loading/empty/error states and price-change calculations
- Crop detail with 14-day chart, min/max/average and ranked market comparison
- Public marketplace search/filter/sort and listing detail with Call/WhatsApp actions

## Phase 2 — farmer tools and completion (implemented)

- Alerts list/badge, create, edit, toggle and delete
- My Listings, listing create/edit, mark Sold and delete
- Profit calculator and ±20% what-if slider
- Final role-route guards, translations and accessibility

Environment setup and end-to-end testing are intentionally deferred until the final testing stage.
