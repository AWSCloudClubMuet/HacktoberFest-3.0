## How to log in

1. Open the app and go to **Log in**.
2. Use a **Demo accounts** button to fill that student's email and password, then click **Log in**.
3. Or type the email and `Demo@1234` yourself.
4. To try a brand-new student, use **Sign up**. There is no email verification.

Log out from the name in the header when you want to switch students.

## Suggested 5-minute walkthrough

1. Log in as **Zainab** and post a gig. Choose **Paid** and submit with the budget empty, then fix it and post.
2. Log out. Log in as **Bilal** and apply to that gig. Apply again and confirm the duplicate is blocked.
3. Log in as **Zainab**, open the same gig, and confirm she cannot apply to her own gig.
4. Log in as **Sara** and apply.
5. Log in as **Zainab**, accept **Bilal**. Sara's application becomes Rejected and the gig moves to **In Progress**.
6. Click **Mark as completed**.
7. As Zainab, leave a review. Log in as Bilal and leave one too. The form disappears after each review.
8. Open both profiles and confirm the average rating and completed-gig count changed.
9. Browse gigs and open **Logo sketch session**. It is stored as Open with a past deadline, so the page shows **Expired** and new applications are closed.

## Rules worth testing

- A student cannot apply to their own gig.
- A student cannot apply twice to the same gig.
- Only the owner can accept, reject, complete, or cancel.
- A gig can be marked Completed only from In Progress.
- Each participant can review the other person once, and only after the gig is Completed.
- A budget is required for Paid gigs and is not accepted for Skill Swap or Free gigs.
- An Open gig whose deadline has passed is shown as Expired.

These accounts are demo-only. They do not contain real personal data.
