    What this protects against, and what it doesn't

    Versioning means an overwrite or delete leaves earlier copies behind. Public access is blocked and the app only needs object-level permissions, so a leaked key can't open the bucket up. Lifecycle rules keep storage costs down.

    It does not cover someone deleting every version, stolen admin credentials, or a regional outage. For those you'd add MFA delete or Object Lock, a second account, and cross-region replication.
