## Our Projects

### 1. Campus Gig & SkillSwap Portal

**Issue:** [#12](https://github.com/AWSCloudClubMuet/HacktoberFest-3.0/issues/12)  
**Built by:** Zainab Muhammad

A student-focused marketplace that enables university students to find campus opportunities, offer services, and exchange skills. It supports paid gigs, free assistance, and skill swaps, helping students collaborate and make better use of their abilities.

**Key Features**

- Create, browse, search, and filter student gigs.
- Support paid, free, and skill-exchange opportunities.
- Submit applications and manage applicants.
- Track gig statuses from Open to In Progress and Completed.
- Manage student profiles and available services.
- Build trust through ratings and reviews.
- Explore the platform using demo users.

**Technology Stack:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, REST API, and JSON-based persistence.

### 2. ReliefHub — Flood Relief Coordination Dashboard

**Issue:** [#10](https://github.com/AWSCloudClubMuet/HacktoberFest-3.0/issues/10)  
**Built by:** Tooba Azizullah

A flood relief coordination dashboard designed to help relief teams manage camps, supplies, urgent needs, dispatches, donations, and volunteers. It provides a centralized view of relief resources to help identify underserved camps and prioritize essential deliveries.

**Key Features**

- Manage relief camps, districts, capacity, and affected families.
- Track camp needs, required quantities, priorities, and fulfilment.
- Manage inventory and highlight low-stock supplies.
- Track dispatches through Pending, In Transit, Delivered, and Cancelled states.
- Validate available stock and restore stock when eligible dispatches are cancelled.
- Record cash and in-kind donations.
- Manage volunteers by skills and camp assignments.
- Display dashboard metrics and fulfilment charts.
- Identify critical gaps where high-priority needs have less than 40% fulfilment.

**Technology Stack:** See the project's individual README for the verified technologies and implementation details.

### 3. Noisy Labels: HelpDeskNet-50K

**Issue:** [#9](https://github.com/AWSCloudClubMuet/HacktoberFest-3.0/issues/9)  
**Built by:** Maryam Muhammad

An AI/ML analysis project investigating how incorrect labels affect text classification performance. The fictional HelpDeskNet-50K dataset contains 50,000 customer-support tickets across six categories: Billing, Refund, Technical, Account Access, Shipping, and Other. The scenario describes a classifier whose test accuracy plateaus at 82%, motivating an investigation into data quality and evaluation reliability.

**Key Features and Analysis**

- Estimate crowd-label noise rates with 95% confidence intervals.
- Compare annotation error rates between experienced and newer crowdworkers.
- Identify systematic patterns in crowd-to-expert labeling disagreements.
- Estimate the potential impact of noisy test labels on reported model accuracy.
- Develop a strategy to prioritize 2,000 tickets for expert relabeling.
- Recommend two training methods that tolerate residual label noise.
- Design a trustworthy, expert-verified evaluation set.
- Document calculations, findings, and recommendations.

**Technology Stack:** See the project's individual README for the verified analysis tools and implementation details.
